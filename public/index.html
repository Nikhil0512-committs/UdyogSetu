⚠️ Vercel Configuration Error

If you are seeing this screen, it means Vercel is incorrectly deploying your app as a "Static Site" instead of a "Next.js" app.

How to fix this in 30 seconds:

  • Go to your Vercel Dashboard
  • Click Settings → General
  • Scroll down to Framework Preset
  • Change it to Next.js and click Save
  • Go to Deployments and click Redeploy
